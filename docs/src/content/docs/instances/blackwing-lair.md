Implementation notes and known behaviors for **Blackwing Lair**.

## Notes

### Ebonroc – Shadowflame Sparks

Shadowflame Sparks from Ebonroc are counted as "pets" of Ebonroc. Damage credit from these sparks is attributed to Ebonroc rather than tracked as a separate entity.
